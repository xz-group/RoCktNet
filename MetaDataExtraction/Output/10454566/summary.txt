FIGURE NUMBER
"Figure 9.9.2 shows the overall CSPC structure, consisting of the 1st and 2nd pre-ampliﬁers, gain-boosting, and a CMOS latch. Variation in VICM and PVT changes the output commonmode voltage of the 1st pre-ampliﬁer. Directly delivering these outputs (Vup,1+, Vup,1-, Vdn,1+, and Vdn,1-) to the latch could lead to decision failure or speed degradation due to the uncontrolled output common-mode voltage. To address this issue, a 2nd pre-ampliﬁer and a gain-boosting circuit were introduced. The 2nd pre-ampliﬁer uses a structure similar to the 1st pre-ampliﬁer, reducing the impact of the output common-mode voltage. Additionally, through positive-feedback-based gain-boosting, the outputs (Vdn,2+, Vdn,2-, Vup,2+, and Vup,2-) are shifted to the 0 or VDD domain, preventing the decision speed degradation at the latch. The CMOS Latch takes inputs from all outputs of the 2nd preampliﬁer without a clock for the self-timed operation. These gain stages help reduce the input-referred noise of the comparator."
