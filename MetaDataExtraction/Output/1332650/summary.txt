# A 12.5Gb/s CMOS BER Test Using a Jitter-Tolerant Parallel CDR Yusuke Ohtomo, Tomoaki Kawamura, Kazuyoshi Nishimura, Masafumi Nogawa, Hiroshi Koizumi, Minoru Togashi

VENUE

FIGURE NUMBER
"Figure 9.5.6 shows the CDR monitor outputs at 12.5Gb/s operation. Jitter characteristics of the parallel CDR are evaluated at the data rate of 10.66Gb/s. Jitter transfer measurement shows -3dB bandwidth of 4MHz and maximum jitter transfer peaking of less than 0.1dB. The measured jitter tolerance (Fig. 9.5.6) exceeds not only the SONET OC-192 jitter tolerance but also the XFP non-data-dependent jitter tolerance. The minimum jitter tolerance for (4-80MHz) sinusoidal jitter exceeds 0.5UIpp (jitter analyzer limit). Acknowledgements: The authors thank Y. Arai, Y. Kitamura and N. Razavi, “A 40-Gb/s Clock and Data Recovery Circuit in 0.18µm CMOS Technology,” ISSCC Dig. Tech. Papers, pp. 242-245, Feb 2003."
