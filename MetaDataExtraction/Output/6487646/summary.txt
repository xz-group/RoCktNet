"Figure 5.1.6 compares our receivers (narrow band) with blocker-tolerant receivers. Comparing our SE receiver with narrow band receivers [1-2] we use a fraction of the area and power to get better linearity and harmonic rejection and comparable noise. Compared with the best wideband receiver [6] we have a worse noise but better linearity, harmonic rejection and area. In addition we consume 20% less power even though [6] uses an SE LNA also making it more sensitive to coupling. Acknowledgements: This work was supported by the European M.
