FIGURE NUMBER
"Figure 25.7.2 shows the ring oscillator implementation. According to extensive simulations of various tuning mechanisms, varactor tuning poses the least phase-noise penalty, a point evidently not previously recognized. The transistor dimensions (W/L = 36μm/0.28μm for both NMOS and PMOS devices) are chosen so as to reduce their flicker noise to the point where the phase-locked phase noise beyond a few hundred kilohertz offset is dominated by white noise. The free-running 2.4GHz VCO produces a phase noise of -121dBc/Hz at 10MHz offset while consuming 3.1mW power."
