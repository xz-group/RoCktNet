"Figure 12.7.3 shows the detailed schematic diagram of the ADCC block. A charge pump adjusts a variable voltage, VCP, to be used by a mono-stable pulse generator to generate the SC waveform with dynamic duty cycle. By default, a finite state machine (FSM) forces the charge pump to either charge or discharge its output capacitor (CCP) until the voltage envelope across the L3C3-tank (VENV) reduces by 100mV. Then, the charge/discharge state changes, which results in VENV to increase, until VENV reaches to its maximum while achieving the optimal RL condition and again reduces by 100mV by transitioning form the optimal condition. This up-and-down cycle ensures that VENV remains close to its peak value, which results in transformation of RL to the optimal load required for high PTE. In the ADCC, VENV is first detected by a passive rectifier, divided by 1.4, and then buffered before being sampled.
