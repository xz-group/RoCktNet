# An 87mA·min Iontophoresis Controller IC with DualMode Impedance Sensor for Patch-Type Transdermal Drug Delivery System Kiseok Song, Unsoo Ha, Jaehyuk Lee, Kyeongryeol Bong, Hoi-Jun Yoo

FIGURE NUMBER
"Figure 6.1.1 shows the basic iontophoresis concept and the proposed patch type iontophoresis system. Stimulation electrodes and differential electrodes are fabricated on the planar-fashionable circuit board (P-FCB) [5] for drug delivery and tissue impedance (ZTIS) measurement, respectively, on the bottom side of the patch. A smart controller IC is directly wire-bonded and a coin battery is attached on the upper side. An external base station turns on the system, programs the stimulation parameters and starts stimulation. The skin temperature, contact and tissue impedances are measured every minute, and stored in the SRAM (4kB) or transmitted to the external base station through the body channel communication (BCC) interface [6]. If there is a failure due to the poor contact or, if the iontophoresis is completed, a LED and buzzer on the patch turn on. The detailed information of the drug delivery can be checked from the stored data or through the BCC. 2006."
