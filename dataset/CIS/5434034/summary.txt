FIGURE NUMBER
"Figure 4.9.2 shows the simplified architecture of the proposed AFIR. In this circuit, 10 interleaved signal paths are used to implement a 16-tap FIR filter. Each tap requires one clock period, 2 periods are added for sampling and 2 periods for resetting the outputs (through a ‘dump’ switch), totaling 20 periods to generate a sample. This interleaved architecture combines well with an interleaved ADC running at Fclk/2, allowing the realization of the output MUX in digital and easing the AFIR filter measurements."
