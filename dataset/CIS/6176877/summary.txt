FIGURE NUMBER
"Fig. 3.3.2. Maximum power per processor is less than 1W/GHz. Low power has been achieved by aggressive clock gating, full custom circuit and layout design techniques, creative floorplanning and leakage management. Photon emission imaging techniques have been employed on previous products in the same technology in order to identify leakage hot spots and die areas that exhibited elevated leakage currents. Size rebalancing among HVT and RVT devices has been performed to reduce overall leakage without affecting frequency margin. Our overall leakage to total non-I/O power ratio averages at 25% at maximum specified case temperature (90C to 85C depending on product bin). In addition to the above mentioned low-power techniques, each core includes the ability to ensure that dynamic power does not exceed a software programmable limit, which is a percentage of maximum possible power. Architectural correlators have been developed that monitor dynamic power during program"
