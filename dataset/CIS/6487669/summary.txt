The latch outputs are buffered by double emitter followers with feedback to reduce transient ringing effects, and preamplified by Q1,2. Grounding the (common) bases of the transistors in the final output stage extends the collector-emitter breakdown voltage of Q11,12 to near BVCBO. A differential output voltage swing beyond 10V could therefore be realized by adding more DA cells and extending the output line.
