FIGURE NUMBER
"Fig. 8.6.2, results in very long interconnections and adds significant RC delays to the search operation. Hence, the leap-frog interconnection with minimal hops is used, as shown in the lower part of Fig. 8.6.2, to shorten the interconnection among gates and maintain the search speed. For power consumption, a TCAM consumes power mainly in 3 parts: clock and control, match line, and search line. Thanks to the advancement of match-line design techniques, the power consumption of the former two has been greatly reduced. This can be seen from the works of the past three years [2, 3, 1], where the power consumption for search lines occupies about 54%, 71%, and 82% of the total power, respectively. To tackle the power consumption problem on search lines, pipelining and hierarchical search lines are used in [4]. However, the area and power overhead caused by the pipeline flip-flops and the clock driver diminishes the usefulness of the approach."
